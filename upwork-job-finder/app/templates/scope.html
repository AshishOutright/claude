Know your Upwork API key scope

Your key is read-only for job postings. It can find and read jobs, which is everything this app needs to search, score and alert. It cannot act on Upwork for you: no submitting proposals, no messaging clients, no reading your profile, contracts or earnings.

✓
Can do
Search jobs · read full job details · read jobs your company posted as a client
✕
Cannot do
Apply / send proposals · message clients · read freelancer profile, contracts, earnings or Connects

The four permissions on your key

Read-only access to job details entities The full detail record of a single job Open one job and read its complete record, with more fields than search results return (for example the full description and job requirements) Available Could enrich the job page and proposal drafts
Read-only access to job postings from client point of view Jobs your own organization posted as a client (GraphQL jobPosting) Only useful if you also hire on Upwork: check status and details of jobs you posted. Does not help find work. Not used

Upwork's API docs also say the "Common Entities – Read-Only Access" scope is required for all calls. It isn't in your screenshot. If searches fail with a permission error after you connect, open your key at upwork.com/developer/keys, add that scope, then reconnect here.

What you can build with this key

  • Job radar. Saved searches that run on a schedule and alert you to new matches by email or Telegram. in this app
  • Fit scoring. Rank jobs by keyword match, budget, client quality and freshness. in this app
  • Client vetting. Skip unverified or zero-hire clients automatically. in this app
  • Proposal drafting. Claude writes a draft from the job text, and you paste it into Upwork yourself. in this app
  • Market research. Track demand for skills (e.g. SuiteCRM vs Salesforce), average budgets and hourly rates over time.
  • Lead sheet. Export matched jobs to your CRM or a spreadsheet for your team to follow up.

What needs a different scope

  • Submitting proposals or applying. Upwork offers proposal APIs, but they need extra scopes that Upwork rarely grants. Apply on upwork.com.
  • Messaging clients and reading chat rooms.
  • Your freelancer profile, stats, Job Success Score or Connects balance.
  • Contracts, time logs, earnings and invoices.
  • Client contact details. Upwork never exposes emails or phone numbers.

To get more access, edit your key at upwork.com/developer/keys and request the extra scopes. Upwork reviews each request by hand.

How this app uses your key

  1. Connect Upwork sends you to Upwork to approve read-only access. The app gets a token, never your password.
  2. Searches call Upwork's GraphQL API with that token. Results are stored in this app's own database.
  3. Your Client ID and Secret stay in the server's environment settings and are never shown in the browser.
  4. Disconnect deletes the stored token. You can also revoke access anytime in your Upwork account settings.
